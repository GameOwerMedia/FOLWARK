FOLWARK

a political ecosystem, after George Orwell
„All animals are equal, but some animals are more equal than others.”
— Animal Farm, 1945
The humans are gone. The farm belongs to the animals — and, quietly, to you. You are the unseen hand: you choose whose kind holds the farmhouse, you pull the levers of propaganda and fear, you decide who eats, who builds, and — when the dogs grow hungry — who becomes the meal. The fields feed the granary, the meadow feeds the herd, the waterhole feeds them all, and the pigs scheme regardless. Two histories will be written: what actually happened, and what the farm is told. Only one of them survives you.
